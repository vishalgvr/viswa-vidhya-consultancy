Our 8 Comprehensive Consultancy Services

From discovering your strengths to boarding your international flight, we ensure every step is transparent, methodical, and stress-free.

01

1. Career Counselling

We conduct comprehensive 1-on-1 discovery dialogues with students and parents. We analyze your academic transcripts, aptitude, budget limitations, and future career vision to design a realistic global study roadmap.

Key Action: Personal Profile Mapping & Diagnostic Assessment
02

2. Course Selection

Identify high-growth undergraduate, postgraduate, medical (MBBS), diploma, or professional programs that align with emerging industry demand, global licensing requirements, and personal interests.

Key Action: Curriculum Review & Industry Employability Analysis
03

3. Country Selection

Help parents and students evaluate the best country fit—whether Mauritius (MBBS with Indian faculty & safe hostels), Germany (Engineering, low tuition & 18-month stay-back), or Hungary (EU-accredited medical degrees).

Key Action: Safety, Budget, Language & Work Rights Comparison
04

4. University Selection

Shortlist officially accredited universities recognized by NMC, WHO, and European ministries. We verify campus hospital affiliations, faculty quality, pass rates, and campus amenities.

Key Action: Accredited Institution Shortlisting & Faculty Vetting
05

5. Application Support

We guide students through document verification, apostille procedures, translation checks, Statement of Purpose (SOP) drafting, and error-free university portal filings before intake deadlines.

Key Action: Dossier Compilation, SOP Refinement & Timely Submissions
06

6. Admission Support

We liaise directly with university international admissions deans, track your application lifecycle, confirm seat allocation, and assist with transparent tuition fee remittance.

Key Action: Official Offer Letter & Guaranteed Seat Confirmation
07

7. Visa Guidance

Comprehensive student visa dossier compilation, sponsorship proof, financial validation, embassy appointment scheduling, and thorough mock interview training for maximum success.

Key Action: Financial Audit, Appointment Booking & Mock Interviews
08

8. Pre-Departure Guidance

Flight ticketing advice, foreign exchange (Forex) cards, packing checklists, airport pickup coordination in Mauritius/Germany/Hungary, and direct hostel room key handover.

Key Action: Airport Pickups, Hostel Allotment & Smooth Landing
