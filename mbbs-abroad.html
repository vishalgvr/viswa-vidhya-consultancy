Admission Requirements

MBBS Eligibility Criteria

Medical admissions abroad follow structured, transparent eligibility standards:

01
10+2 with PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB for general category (40% for reserved categories).
02
NEET Qualification
Must qualify the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) in the current or previous 2 years.
03
Age Criteria
Candidate must be at least 17 years old on or before 31st December of the admission year.
04
Language Requirement (No IELTS for Mauritius)
Mauritius requires NO IELTS for medical admissions. For Hungary, medium of instruction certificate or IELTS is evaluated.
