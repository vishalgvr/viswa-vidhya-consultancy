Mauritius

Top Choice for MBBS & Higher Education

Mauritius is a serene, disciplined, and safe island nation situated in the Indian Ocean. Renowned for its welcoming culture and English-medium institutions, it has emerged as the most favored destination for Indian students pursuing medical and undergraduate degrees.

Language Req.
No IELTS Required
Intakes
Feb / Mar & Sep / Oct
  • Very low crime rate & peaceful campus environment
  • Majority Indian faculty members and student community
  • Higher FMGE passing average + 1st Rank holder
  • Separate secure hostel accommodation for boys & girls

Germany

Global Engine of Engineering & Innovation

Germany stands at the pinnacle of scientific research, technology, and engineering in Europe. With state-of-the-art laboratory facilities and strong industry-academia partnerships, German public universities provide tuition-free or extremely affordable education.

Tuition Status
Low to Zero (Public Unis)
Work Permit
18-Month Stay-Back Visa
  • World-renowned public universities with global prestige
  • Hub for automotive, robotics, AI, and industrial tech
  • English-taught Bachelor & Master degrees available
  • Student discounts on public transport & health insurance

Hungary

European Academic Heritage & Medical Degrees

Hungary provides high-quality European Union standard education in the heart of Central Europe. With historical medical faculties, vibrant student life in cities like Budapest, Debrecen, and Szeged, it is an esteemed gateway to Europe.

Degree Recognition
EU, NMC & WHO Accredited
Scholarships
Stipendium Hungaricum
  • Centuries-old medical universities with advanced clinical rotations
  • Schengen visa access across 27+ European nations
  • Affordable European cost of living and tuition packages
  • Direct career pathways across Central & Western Europe
